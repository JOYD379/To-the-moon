Chapter 1: Why We Sent Robots to the Moon

🚀

The Mission Begins!

Long before humans ever stepped on the Moon, scientists on Earth had big questions: "Is the Moon's ground soft like quicksand? Can a spaceship land safely?" To find out, NASA built smart robotic explorers and sent them off into space on giant rockets!

🛠️ How They Worked

Robots used radio antennas, solar panels, and camera tubes to talk to scientists 238,900 miles away!

🪐 Why Left Behind

Rockets needed to be lightweight to launch back to Earth. So heavy parts stayed on the Moon forever!

⚡ Still Useful Today?

Yes! Mirrors left by Apollo are still hit by lasers today, teaching us how the Moon moves!

Apollo 11 Lunar Module

Landed: July 20, 1969

🛸
✓
🎯 Why Was It Sent?

To land astronauts safely and serve as their cozy shelter on the Moon.

⚙️ How It Worked

Powered by rocket engines and batteries, it split in two when leaving!

🌕 Why Is It Left Here?

The bottom stage stayed behind to act as a launchpad for the top part!

💡 Still Useful Today?

Scientists study how its metals survived solar radiation for future Moon bases!
